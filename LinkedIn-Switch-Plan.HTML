THE FOUR STRATEGIC ROUTES

ROUTE 01

THE BIG SWITCH

OBSERVATIONHighly visible details are often treated as afterthoughts.
TENSIONThe switch is small, but it is one of the most visible and frequently interacted-with elements in a room.
PROVOCATIONWHY SHOULD THE SMALLEST DETAIL GET THE LEAST ATTENTION?
EVIDENCEVisually-led modular design options.
ROUTE 02

NEW ON THE WALL. NOT NEW TO ELECTRICALS.

OBSERVATIONSturlite Switch is new, but Sturlite itself is not new to electricals.
TENSIONNew product category. Established parent ecosystem.
PROVOCATIONTHE SWITCH IS NEW. THE ELECTRICAL EXPERIENCE BEHIND IT ISN'T.
EVIDENCE14+ years | 19+ states | 14,000+ dealers
ROUTE 03

LOOK BEYOND THE SWITCH.

OBSERVATIONA switch appears visually simple while the decision involves safety, quality, installation and engineering.
TENSIONWhat people see is simple. What makes the product trustworthy isn't.
PROVOCATIONA SWITCH CAN LOOK RIGHT AND STILL RAISE THE WRONG QUESTIONS.
EVIDENCETechnical specifications, QC processes and verified product information.
RESEARCH-LED

THE FINAL FINISH.

OBSERVATIONPeople spend considerable time debating paint, furniture and tiles, but switches can remain an afterthought.
TENSIONA visible design element can receive invisible consideration.
PROVOCATIONDON'T LET AN AFTERTHOUGHT FINISH YOUR WALL.
IDEAReposition switches as interior design hardware.
